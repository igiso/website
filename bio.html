CAREER AND RECOGNITION

Short bio

Kyriakos Kousoulides (Cyprus, 1985) is a visual and media artist with a background in philosophy and computer science with over 10 years of experience in large-scale software development & heuristics. He Graduated from Athens National School of Fine Arts, Greece and further acquired an MA in Computational Arts from Goldsmith’s University, London. His work has been Curated and Presented in Prominant Spaces in Cyprus, Greece, China, United Kingdom and Germany. He has created the projection mappings and 3d video Interactive Environments for the Technologically Groundbraking Public Performance At the Opening Ceremony of the European Capital of Culture Pafos2017 as a member of the creative team led by the Walk the Plank theatre company. Since 2019 he teaches at C.U.T (Cyprus University of Technology).

    At the core of my research and artistic practice lies a deep exploration of the Human Condition, encompassing both our physical and animalistic nature, as well as our inherent self-awareness. This profound inquiry has sparked a philosophical journey through life, leading to a profound realization of mortality. Schopenhauer's philosophy of the indifference of nature to human existence informs my artistic exploration as well. I reflect on the inherent tension between our human experiences and the larger forces of nature, underscoring the fragility and transience of our existence within the vastness of the natural world. To narrow down this vast subject, I focus on what I refer to as micro-thematics. Within these thematic explorations, the joys experienced by some individuals can coexist with feelings of melancholy, loneliness, and hopelessness felt by others. I strive to capture specific moments in time that possess a multifaceted complexity. In seemingly ordinary everyday events, love, compassion, and selflessness can be juxtaposed with hate, malice, and ego. Similarly, the contrasting emotions of depression, isolation, and hopelessness can intertwine with the joys of life. All these elements find expression within a single picture. Art Critic Professor Thanasis Moutsopoulos has described my work as having the character of a complete, integrated, and autonomous world. This intention resonates deeply with me as I strive to create a McLuhan-style immersive anti-environment that invites viewers on a visceral journey, allowing them to connect with their emotions and delve into profound thoughts. Without hesitation, I engage with even the most superficial attributes of our condition. I revel in satirizing vanity-driven politics and employing irony to comment on the peculiar zeitgeist of our era. As a result, my body of work encompasses a diverse range of media, spanning from Video Art and Interactive Projections to small drawings and sketches. This blend of art and technology emerges organically from my constant desire to remain inspired and true to my objectives, employing the most appropriate tools for each artistic endeavor. Derrida's contemplation on death resonates strongly in my work, as I confront the inevitable finitude of life and its impact on our perception of self and the world. Drawing from his insights, I strive to evoke a profound sense of contemplation and introspection within viewers, inviting them to question and explore their own mortality. I often adopt a minimalistic approach, placing the message and idea at the forefront, unless a more realistic treatment is essential to the artwork. Through my art, I invite viewers to engage with the depths of their own humanity, reflecting on the complexities of existence and our relationship with both ourselves and the world around us.

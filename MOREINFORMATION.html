ABOUT

Kyriacos Kousoulides is a Visual and media artist with a background in philosophy and computer science. He has extensive experience in Creative Computation and more than 10 Years experience in large scale software development. Deep knowledge of C++, Objective-C, QT, VBA, 3D Visuals, JavaScript and many other technologies. He is an Open Frameworks Contributor, His work has been selected for the Artic Vault Software code preservation initiative. Since 2009 his work has been exhibited in more than 30 prominent exhibition places and galleries in Germany, United Kingdom, China, Greece and Cyprus alongside artists like Günter Brus, Ulay, Kimsooja, Anne Wilson, Christina Lucas, Diana Wood Conroy and many more.
